At the time of writing, the new Express/Handlebars app is running intermittently. In the process of building:

- Started using Vagrant for testing. Though this site is incredibly simple, it feels downright luxurious to mock up a VPS in seconds. Check out [Justin Weissig's series on Vagrant](https://sysadmincasts.com/episodes/42-crash-course-on-vagrant-revised), as well as any of his other screencasts.
- The performance and accessibility improvements are pretty astounding. Trimming the fat feels great. (Would highly recommend.)
