<p>Well it’s certainly been a while. A recap:</p>
<ul>
<li>I got an honest-to-goodness, full-time job. I work with data. It’s nice.</li>
<li>I learned R for the data stuff. It’s a funny language, but the community’s good and there’s (mostly) no weird, Python-ish whitespace.</li>
<li>I also got a whole lot better at stuff on the web. Started fiddling with all kinds of JavaScript frameworks<a href="#fn:1" title="see footnote">[1]</a> and interactive visualisations for work.</li>
</ul>
<p>So, a year of learning. And it’s been great! I needed some time to meet people and soak in some new ideas. But, I’ve missed blogging. I have a bad habit of sitting on things until I’m convinced they’re perfect—which <em>never</em> happens—so I’m trying to get better at putting things out there and letting you figure it out.</p>
<p>I’m typically in the camp of “tools don’t matter”, but the previous version of this site never felt quite right; publishing a post meant moving files wrapped up in <a href="http://yaml.org">YAML</a> over FTP to my server. Not bad, just… kludgy. I’ll do a full write-up at some point when I’ve had a chance to tidy up some of my mess, but this incarnation comes much closer to meeting my own needs. Which means I now don’t have an excuse to be on here.</p>
<p>Anyways, if you’re still listening, thanks; and if you’re new, well, also thanks—and say hi<a href="#fn:2" title="see footnote">[2]</a>.</p>
